### Recorded, not fixed (each needs an Owner decision)

- FFR-D1 (P3). An expected outcome that already held before a faithfully reproduced application is still recorded `success`; no observation is made before the application. Requiring a not-held → held transition would exclude guard-type outcomes ("the tests still pass"). Use statistics are informational (`CognitiveMemory.Utility` is `causal_benefit_unmeasured`); nothing ranks or promotes on them.
- FFR-D2 (P3, architecture). Maintainer candidates (`cognition_candidates`: `read_knowledge`, the Start index) and native records (`cognition_nodes`: `assess_cognition`, Governor, Experience) share the assertion type, host bindings, check evidence, fences and privacy purge, but not one lifecycle, conflict detection or read path. Neither can show a claim as `current` without a passing host-bound check; a contradiction between unverified claims of the two families is not reported as a conflict.
- FFR-D3 (P3, scale). A record accepts at most eight uses in its lifetime, and fewer when its definition and observations are large: the 64 KiB of a node (ADR-0023) hold every use with its feedback (FFR-017 makes that bound safe, it does not lift it). Moving uses to their own table would lift both bounds; it is a schema change.
- FFR-D4 (P3, compatibility). The fixes hold for records the fixed code writes. A store written by the unmerged candidate itself is not converted; probed on a scratch copy of the review tip that writes a record as the candidate did (`probes/candidate-store-d5542d4.*`). (a) An Experience record created there keeps its random owner. The guard of FFR-014 reads the fence of the owner the identity names, and it replaced the lineage guard of FFR-012, so it does not cover that record: after the deletion of a consumer erased it, the origin extracted the same identity again as a fresh candidate. (b) A record the candidate validated over a failed origin action (the FFR-008 defect) no longer decodes. Reading it, `RecallExperiences`, `GovernorAnalyze` and `MaintainCognitiveMemory` then fail `cognitive store is incomplete or corrupt` for the project, also after a restart; reading, assessing or adopting another record by id, a run and reopening the Engine are not affected; deleting the origin session of that record erases it, and the three calls succeed again. No other fix narrows what a stored record may be. A store migrated from `main` holds no cognition record and is not affected. The candidate was never merged or released and its migrations were never published, so such a store exists only in development. Decision for the Owner: recreate such a store, or ask for its conversion (a data migration, not authorized in this review).
